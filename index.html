Zahlenkönig

A number puzzle for the browser: you get two to four numbers and a target. Reach the target, and use every number exactly once.

  • Plus, minus, times and divide — you choose which operators turn up.
  • Brackets are yours to place: drag a number onto a bracket edge to pull it inside.
  • A hint plays the next move when you get stuck.
  • From first-graders to mental-arithmetic veterans: two to four numbers, four target sizes.
  • Free, no sign-up, no ads — installable and playable offline.

This page needs JavaScript to play. With it switched on, the game starts straight away.
